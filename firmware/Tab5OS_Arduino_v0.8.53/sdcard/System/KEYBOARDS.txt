Touch and USB keyboard layouts - Tab5OS

keyboards.json retains the lower/upper/symbols layers. The hid field is optional:
[HID_usage, normal_text, Shift_text, AltGr_text, CapsLock_letter, dead_key_mask].
The mask uses bits 0/1/2 for normal/Shift/AltGr. Accents: circumflex (^), diaeresis,
acute, grave and tilde.
Missing entries retain the US behavior, or French behavior for fr-FR.
The additional USB tables come from the XKB gb/de/es/it/pt/br/us layouts.
The available layouts are listed in keyboards.json. Dutch uses physical US keys.
The interface language and keyboard layout are separate choices.
