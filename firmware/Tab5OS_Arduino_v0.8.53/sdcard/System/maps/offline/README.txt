OFFLINE MAPS

Copy here every .t5map volume prepared on your PC using tools/Cartes_Tab5.cmd.
Available maps: OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap.
Choose a city or coordinates, a radius and zoom levels; estimate before downloading.
The PC program can copy packs directly here when you select the SD card root.
In Tab5OS, select OSM Standard or OpenTopoMap. Attribution remains visible.
Volumes default to 1,900 MiB (2,000 MiB maximum), below the SDK 2 GiB seek limit. Up to 128 packs on the SD card, across all map styles.
Detailed guide: tools/CARTES_PC.md in the firmware archive.
